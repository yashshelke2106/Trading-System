# The way to get profitable — measured, ranked, honest

**Date:** 2026-07-31 · On Rs1,00,000 paper book

## The one-line answer

Profit is already reachable, and it comes from **beta, not alpha**. The highest
honest expected return is a book tilted to the validated equity sleeve, with the
options sleeve kept small as a cheap, tail-capped bet on the volatility premium
that the forward paper month is testing.

`edge_required = cost / (target + stop)` — you cannot raise edge (13 hunts
rejected), so profit comes from the two things you CAN control: harvest the
premia that are real, and stop paying for the ones that aren't.

**50/50 has the same expected return as 100%-equity ONLY if the condor edge is
real — and it is unproven.** Every rupee moved from equity into the condor
sleeve today trades a certain premium for an uncertain one, plus a vol tail.

## The way to get profitable — concretely

1. **Tilt to the validated sleeve.** For pure profit-maximisation the evidence
   says overweight equity. The 50/50 you specified is defensible only as a
   structure you're deliberately using to *forward-test the condor* — which is
   exactly what the paper month is for. Keep it if that's the intent; tilt to
   ~75/25 or higher if the intent is "make money now".

2. **Treat the condor sleeve as a call option on the VRP.** ~0 EV today, cheap
   to run, tail capped by the wings. If the paper month (and the months after)
   show the +2.2%/trade holding, its EV becomes real and the tilt back toward
   50/50 earns its keep. Until then, size it small.

3. **Do not run the PF-0.44 scanner with capital.** It now finds trades again
   (data fixed) but they are the net-negative signals the journal already
   measured. Running it is negative expectancy — the opposite of the goal.

4. **Cut cost, not corners.** A real broker (Zerodha/Dhan, ~0.03% delivery vs
   the 0.20% modelled) is pure expectancy on the equity sleeve — no research
   risk, no overfitting, just less leakage.

## The honest ceiling

Profitable: **yes — ~8–12%/yr, beta-driven, positive expectancy, low drama.**
That compounds to something real over years. It is Buffett-tier as a *rate*,
and it is the genuine answer to "get profitable".

It is not, and cannot be, the 15,000%/150×/get-rich number. Those require an
edge that 13 registered hunts, a 2,700-config search, and a closed-form proof
all show is absent at retail scale. "Get profitable" is reachable. "Get rich
quick" is a different, measured-impossible request.

The way to get profitable is to stop looking for the edge and start harvesting
the premium — which is what the allocation engine already does. Deploy it,
give it time, let the condor prove or disprove itself on paper alongside.
